2. Incident Review & Alarm Response



This process is event-driven, triggered by an incident report or an alarm activation.



A. Alarm Activation (Section 5.1)

Immediate Acknowledgment: Upon a high-priority alarm (e.g., intrusion, panic button), acknowledge the alert in the Verkada Command interface within 15 seconds.

Dual Verification: Use the corresponding camera feed (automatically linked) to visually verify the cause of the alarm.

B. Incident Review and Verification (Section 4.2)

Access Feeds: Immediately access the camera feeds relevant to the area upon receiving an alarm or incident report.

Review Footage: Review footage starting 5 minutes prior to the reported event time to establish context.

Verification: Determine if the activity is a genuine security threat, a false alarm, or a known/authorized event (e.g., maintenance).

C. Dispatch and Escalation (Section 5.2, 5.3)

D. Footage Export & Archiving (If Incident is Verified) (Section 4.3)

Preservation: Immediately bookmark the relevant video segment in the Verkada Command platform.

Export: Export the required footage in the designated file format (e.g., MP4) and save it to the secure Incident Folder.

Chain of Custody: Log the export details (operator name, date/time, clip duration, file path) in the Incident Management System (IMS).
